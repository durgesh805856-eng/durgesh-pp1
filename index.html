About Our College

ABC College of Technology was established with the vision of creating skilled professionals for the modern world. Our college provides a combination of academic education, practical training, industry exposure and extracurricular activities.


The college has experienced faculty members, modern laboratories, computer labs, library, smart classrooms, sports facilities and placement support.
